## Brand & Style

This design system embodies extreme minimalism rooted in organic calm, quiet contemplation, and an unhurried digital atmosphere. Drawing inspiration from tactile paper stationery, Japanese domestic architecture, and high-end editorial book design, it strips away cognitive clutter to create a restorative workspace.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Thoughtful professionals, knowledge workers, and mindful operators seeking relief from dense, loud, high-stimulus SaaS dashboards.
- **Tone:** Tranquil, grounded, deliberate, warm, and protective of focus.
- **Design Movement:** Organic Warm Minimalism. The interface rejects synthetic gloss, harsh contrasts, stark tech-pure whites, and vibrating saturated accents in favor of natural cream undertones, soft tactile delineation, and breathable layout rhythms.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by soft mineral and paper hues. Every shade carries a subtle yellow-red warm undertone, avoiding pure cool greys entirely.

- **Canvas / Page Background:** `#F6F3EE` (Warm ivory)
- **Surfaces & Cards:** `#FCFAF7` (Soft cream)
- **Sidebar & Structural Layer:** `#EFEAE2` (Deeper sand)
- **Hairline Divider / Card Border:** `#E9E4DB` (Muted paper edge)
- **Primary Text:** `#2E2B27` (Warm charcoal)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes open, unhurried cadences with wide vertical tracking and relaxed line heights. 

- **Headlines:** `DM Sans` supplies balanced, clean modernism with soft geometric warmth. Titles never exceed `500` or `600` weight to prevent aggressive emphasis.
- **Body & Controls:** `Manrope` provides open counters, tall x-height, and neutral legibility across long-form content, metadata, and form controls.
- **Rhythm & Flow:** Paragraphs and reading containers stay under 68 characters per line. Vertical margins between text blocks use generous, airy spacing to invite calm reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on generous negative space as an active architectural element. The interface mimics a clean wooden table with arranged sheets of parchment.

### Grid & Density
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed canvas max-width of `1280px` or `1440px` with expansive outer margins (`margin`: `3rem`). Column gutters (`gutter`: `1.5rem`) separate content without explicit vertical stroke dividers.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid down to `768px`, outer margins scale to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single primary stack, edge-to-edge breathing room with `1.25rem` outer margin.

### Hierarchy & Padding
- Internal card padding uses generous bounds (`space-lg` / `2rem` on desktop, `space-md` / `1.25rem` on mobile).
- Elements are spaced far enough apart that visible dividers and lines are largely unnecessary; proximity and background color differences alone convey groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through physical, tonal layering rather than dramatic artificial drop shadows.

- **Stacking Surfaces:**
  - Base level: Ivory canvas `#F6F3EE`
  - Structural navigation: Inset or flush sand `#EFEAE2`
  - Floating items & interactive modules: Soft cream `#FCFAF7`
- **Ambient Shadows:** Standard box-shadows are suppressed. Where subtle elevation is mandatory (e.g., floating cards or modal dialogs), use an imperceptible, warm-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 43, 39, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(46, 43, 39, 0.02);`
- **Separation:** Boundaries rely primarily on contrast between `#F6F3EE` and `#FCFAF7`, supported by a crisp 1px hairline border (`#E9E4DB`).
- **Dark Mode Elevation:** Depth is conveyed by stepping luminance upward from base `#1F1D1A` to panels `#2A2723` with `#36332E` hairlines.

## Shapes

The geometric signature balances structured architectural clarity with organic comfort.

- **Panels & Cards:** Fixed `14px` border-radius (`rounded-[14px]` / `rounded-2xl`). This curvature provides an organic, tactile card quality without feeling toy-like.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Consistent `10px` to `12px` roundedness to echo card corners softly.
- **Status Pills & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to immediately signal categorical meta-information distinct from rectangular content containers.
- **Icons:** Thin, refined `1.5px` stroke weight with rounded caps and joins, mirroring the gentle contours of the typography and corner treatments.

## Components

### Single Primary Action Principle
Each screen permits only one solid primary button. Secondary and tertiary options must recede into ghost or subtle outline variations.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#6F8F7F` background with `#FCFAF7` text. Smooth transition to `#5F7F70` on hover. Padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`, rounded `10px`, no drop shadow.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, hairline border in `#E9E4DB`, `#2E2B27` text. Hover shifts background to `#EFEAE2`.
- **Ghost:** Text-only in `#7A746B`, hover state transitions to `#2E2B27` with no background shift.

### Status Badges (Pills)
- Rendered with `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`, `border-radius: 9999px`, using `label-md` typography.
- Built strictly with low-saturation pastel backgrounds and deeper tone-on-tone text:
  - Active: `#6F8F7F` on `#E4EDE8`
  - Renewal Due: `#C98F4F` on `#F6E9D6`
  - Overdue: `#BC6A5C` on `#F5E1DC`
  - Lapsed: `#9A948B` on `#EAE6DF`
  - In Talks: `#7C97AE` on `#E1EAF1`

### Cards & Panels
- Soft cream background (`#FCFAF7`) wrapped with a 1px border (`#E9E4DB`).
- Internal padding of `1.5rem` to `2rem`. Header and body segments flow seamlessly without horizontal dividing lines whenever possible.

### Input Fields
- Background matches card or surface (`#FCFAF7` or `#FFFFFF`), bordered with `#E9E4DB`.
- Rounded `10px`, height `44px`, text in `body-md`.
- Focus state: Hairline border sharpens to `#6F8F7F` with a soft, diffused outer glow `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(111, 143, 127, 0.15)`. No heavy black rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 18px controls with a 1.5px border in `#C8C2B7`.
- Selected state fills with `#6F8F7F` featuring a thin, delicate white inner mark.

### Lists & Tables
- Borderless rows that rely on generous row height (`56px` to `64px`) and alternating subtle row hover fills (`#F6F3EE`).
- Hairline bottom borders (`#E9E4DB`) applied only between major table sections.
